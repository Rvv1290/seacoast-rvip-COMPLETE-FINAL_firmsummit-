1.2  What RVIP is

RVIP is a pre-arranged, reservation-based premium ground-transportation service. You book in the app in advance by choosing a pickup date and time. There is no on-demand (“as soon as possible”) option and no fixed limit on how far ahead you may book. Booking as far ahead of pickup as you can is recommended: it gives RVIP and the carrier the best chance to confirm a driver and vehicle for your Trip.

There are no street hails. Drivers may not pick up anyone who has not booked through the app.

There is no personal-vehicle ridesharing. Every Trip is performed by a licensed carrier in a commercially insured for-hire vehicle with a professional driver. No Trip on RVIP is performed in a personal vehicle.

There is no surge pricing. The price you are quoted before you book is the price.

It includes the traffic normally expected at that time and RVIP's published service-hours rate for late-night and early-morning pickups, and it never responds to real-time demand. If you change the pickup time, the app shows the updated all-in price before you book. The Pricing Policy explains how the price is calculated.

Reserved hours and the booked reserved-time rate or waiting charge remain unchanged; only the affected driving component is recalculated, and the reservation keeps its agreed held-service or return structure.

RVIP must verify an inbound Boston (BOS) flight for that date and a pickup at or after its scheduled arrival, and you use the airport-designated limousine pickup area and current terminal instructions.
