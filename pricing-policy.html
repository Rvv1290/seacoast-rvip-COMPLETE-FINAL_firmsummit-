Pricing, Payment, Cancellation & Refund Policy

Seacoast RVIP LLC  ·  Effective October 31, 2026

Issued by Seacoast RVIP LLC, a New Hampshire limited liability company based in Hampton, NH. See all legal documents.

This policy governs RVIP reservation pricing, payment and refunds. Seacoast RVIP LLC arranges the reservation and administers payment. The transportation provider shown before payment performs the ride; it may be Seacoast RVIP LLC or an identified partner carrier. Read this policy with the Rider Terms of Service.

Section 1
The price shown to you

Fare, taxes and total. The quoted fare includes tolls and applicable airport fees; these are not added again at payment. Any legally applicable sales tax is identified separately within the disclosed total before you agree to the reservation or pay, and is identified on the receipt and any PDF receipt. Do not interpret the absence of a sales-tax charge on one Trip as a promise that every Trip or jurisdiction is tax-exempt. Any future change to tax treatment will be disclosed before agreement and will not authorize an undisclosed charge.

The total price displayed for the selected reservation includes mandatory charges known and calculable at that stage, including applicable taxes and mandatory booking or processing components. RVIP does not use surge pricing under this policy. Surge pricing means a price that rises or falls with real-time demand, driver availability, weather or emergencies; RVIP fares never respond to those conditions. A tip is optional and is not a mandatory part of the fare.

The fare can reflect the route, distance, the driving time expected for the scheduled pickup date and time (including the traffic normally expected then), vehicle or service selected, the published service-hours rate, and applicable airport or other disclosed components. Mandatory charges are not added only at the final payment step. If a rider requests an additional stop, changed route, waiting time or another additional service, RVIP presents the additional price or an objectively stated method of calculation before the rider agrees to it. A carrier cannot independently impose an undisclosed platform charge.

Service-hours rate. Late-night and early-morning pickups are priced at a fixed, published service-hours rate set by the scheduled pickup time in Eastern Time. Standard hours are 5:00 a.m. to 10:59 p.m. For a pickup from 11:00 p.m. to 12:59 a.m., the fare is up to 6 percent higher than the same trip in standard hours. For a pickup from 1:00 a.m. to 4:59 a.m., the fare is up to 10 percent or up to $8 higher, whichever is greater. Expected traffic and the service-hours rate together never raise the transportation charge more than 15 percent above the standard price for that route and vehicle, meaning its price in standard hours with no traffic delay. The rate is the same for every rider and does not depend on a rider's identity, device, location history or purchase history. It is part of the all-in price shown before booking, is not a separate fee and does not change after booking. If the rider changes the pickup time, the app shows the updated all-in price before the rider books.

Section 2
Provider information and payment

Before continuing to payment, the reservation details identify the actual transportation provider and necessary vehicle information. The continuation acknowledgment states that the rider acknowledges that provider and the reservation details. A second checkbox is not required on that card. Account and booking agreement controls remain separate.

For a partner trip, the carrier appoints Seacoast RVIP LLC as its payment-collection agent. Payment received by the authorized agent for the reservation satisfies the corresponding amount owed to the carrier. A rider is not required to pay again because of a failure to remit funds between RVIP, a processor and the carrier. Stripe and related payment services process the transaction subject to the applicable payment authorization.

A reservation or driver acceptance is not proof of successful payment. The driver may arrive before payment, but the trip must not start until the server confirms payment. The rider and driver displays must not label an unsuccessful or unconfirmed payment as paid. If payment fails, the rider may retry through the authorized payment flow or contact support; the driver must not collect an unauthorized substitute payment.

Section 3
Tips and partner allocation

Tips are voluntary. One hundred percent of tips is allocated to the performing driver. RVIP takes no platform fee from tips. For partner service, the standard allocation is 82 percent of fare to the carrier, 18 percent to RVIP and all tips to the driver through the carrier. That allocation does not add a charge to the rider's displayed price.

Section 4
Cancellation before pickup

A rider may cancel before pickup without a cancellation fee. RVIP refunds the full prepaid fare and tip for that canceled reservation. The current policy does not impose a no-show fee for an unperformed ride. Any future cancellation or no-show charge applies only prospectively after clear disclosure and the required acceptance; it cannot be applied retroactively to an existing reservation.

If RVIP or the carrier cancels and no replacement service is accepted, the rider receives a full refund of the prepaid fare and tip. If a different carrier is proposed, RVIP identifies the replacement and obtains the applicable acknowledgment before payment or performance. Existing payments must be reconciled so the rider is not charged twice. A rider need not accept a materially changed reservation instead of a refund for canceled service.

Section 5
Changes and interrupted service

A requested change is confirmed only when it is accepted and any price difference is disclosed and agreed; a checkout completion screen alone is not confirmation. These pricing rules apply only where the compatible modification feature is enabled; other changes go to support.

A date or time change that does not change the pickup, destination or route keeps the agreed fare, whether paid or unpaid. A later time-only edit does not reprice an already-agreed reservation.

For an unpaid reservation, an approved address change sets the fare to the actual recalculated fare; the $15.00 waiver does not apply and no modification payment is taken at that point.

For a paid reservation, an approved address change that increases the fare by $15.00 or less is waived; if the increase is more than $15.00, the rider reviews and pays the entire increase before the change is confirmed.

An eligible paid address change that reduces the fare refunds the full fare difference to the original payment method or methods; a fare adjustment does not reduce or charge a fee against any tip.

Reserved hours and the booked reserved-time or waiting rate are unchanged; only the affected driving component is recalculated. If a ride is not completed, RVIP evaluates the actual service performed, the reason for interruption and applicable consumer rights, and no provision permits retaining payment that law requires to be refunded.

Section 6
Damage and exceptional costs

A damage or cleaning claim requires evidence of the event and reasonable actual costs attributable to the rider. Normal wear, ordinary cleaning and unsupported penalties are not damage claims. RVIP provides the basis and evidence of a proposed charge and at least seven days to respond before resolving a disputed claim, except where law requires a different process. A claim does not authorize an undisclosed automatic charge or waive the rider's legal defenses.

Section 7
Refund timing and disputes

Refunds for canceled reservations are issued automatically to the original payment method. Approved refunds are submitted promptly to the original payment method unless a lawful alternative is agreed. Banks and payment providers control posting times; refunds often appear within five to ten business days, but that period is not guaranteed. Contact support if a refund is not received.

Report a billing concern to info@seacoastrvip.com with the reservation identifier and a brief explanation. RVIP aims to acknowledge ordinary billing inquiries within five business days. Please report issues promptly, preferably within 60 days, but that request does not shorten statutory rights or payment-provider dispute periods. Do not send a complete card number. You retain applicable rights to dispute a transaction and seek lawful remedies.

Section 8
Receipts and changes

A receipt or payment record identifies the transaction, fare, tip, payment status and provider information needed to understand the charge. Corrections preserve an audit trail. Material pricing-policy changes receive prospective notice; no later policy increases the price of an already agreed reservation without the rider's lawful agreement.
