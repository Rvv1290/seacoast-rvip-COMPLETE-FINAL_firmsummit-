Booking
Booking a ride

All Seacoast RVIP rides are pre-arranged — there's no on-demand hailing and no surge pricing. You can book:

  • Online at seacoastrvip.com
  • In the RVIP Rider app (coming soon to iOS and Android)
  • By phone at (978) 255-3140

You'll see your fare before you confirm. For airport pickups, include your flight number so we can track delays.

Changes
Changing or canceling a trip

Need to change a pickup time, address, or passenger count? Contact us as early as you can — email for future trips, phone for anything happening today.

Cancellation terms, timing windows, and any applicable fees are set out in our Terms of Service. If your plans change, tell us as soon as possible and we'll do our best to accommodate you.
Billing
Payments, receipts and business travel

We accept major cards and secure digital payment. After each completed trip you'll receive a receipt with the fare breakdown.

  • Need a receipt again? Email us with the trip date and we'll resend it.
  • Business or corporate travel? Corporate accounts get consolidated statements, NET-30 invoicing and expense-ready receipts — see corporate accounts.
  • Question about a charge? Email us the trip date and the amount and we'll review it with you.
Apps
RVIP Rider & RVIP Driver apps

RVIP Rider lets you book, track your driver live, save favorite places and keep your receipts in one place.

RVIP Driver is for our professional drivers — assigned trips, navigation, earnings and compliance documents.

Having trouble signing in, booking, or with notifications? Email info@seacoastrvip.com and tell us which app, your device (iPhone or Android), and what happened — screenshots help a lot.

Account
Your account and privacy
  • Reset your password: use "Forgot Password" on the sign-in screen, or email us.
  • Update your details: change your name, phone or email in your profile, or ask us to update them.
  • Delete your account: see Delete Your Account for how to request deletion of your RVIP Rider or RVIP Driver account.
  • Privacy questions: read our Privacy Policy or email us about a privacy request.
Safety
Safety and lost items

If you have an urgent safety concern during a trip, call (978) 255-3140 immediately. In an emergency, always call 911 first.

Left something behind? Email or call us with the trip date and a description — we'll contact the driver and arrange its return.
