Electronic Signature Policy and Consent

SEACOAST RVIP, LLC
Company status. Seacoast RVIP, LLC is a New Hampshire limited liability company, formed on October 29, 2026 (New Hampshire Business ID 1045034), that owns and operates the RVIP Rider and RVIP Driver apps and the website at seacoastrvip.com.

Last updated: November 1, 2026. This version applies to you from when you accept it in the app or on the website. Website adoption clarification, 2026-11-04.

Platform consistency. This policy is the same master text for the RVIP apps and the website. The version shown when you accept is the version in effect. Current versions are published in the apps and on seacoastrvip.com.

This policy governs electronic delivery, acknowledgment and signature of RVIP records. Seacoast RVIP, LLC preserves the exact record accepted, the identity and authority of the signer, the method of assent and the time of the transaction. Authentication alone is not acceptance of every document. It covers acceptances and acknowledgments in the RVIP apps and on the website at seacoastrvip.com, including clickwrap and checkbox assent on web booking and corporate booking portal pages.

1 Consent to electronic records

By affirmatively accepting electronic delivery after being given access to this notice, you consent to receive and sign electronically the agreements, disclosures, notices and transaction records identified in the applicable workflow. Where law requires consumer consent under the E-SIGN Act, RVIP obtains that consent in a manner that reasonably demonstrates your ability to access the electronic form to be used. Merely displaying a notice is not sufficient when affirmative consent is required.

You need an internet-connected device, a current supported browser or supported RVIP app, a functioning email address, and software capable of opening and saving the provided document format, including PDF when used. You may save or print records for your files. RVIP provides notice of a material technical change creating a risk that you cannot access or retain future records and obtains renewed consent when required.

2 Paper copies and withdrawal

You may request a paper copy or withdraw electronic-delivery consent by contacting info@seacoastrvip.com. RVIP does not charge for an ordinary copy request under this policy. Withdrawal applies prospectively and does not invalidate a prior lawful signature or an obligation already incurred. Some app- or website-based services may not be available without necessary electronic records; RVIP explains the consequence and any available alternative rather than treating withdrawal as a waiver of legal rights. Keep contact information current through your account or support.

3 How you accept

Riders accept the Rider Terms of Service and the Privacy Policy by an affirmative action, such as checking a box or equivalent clickwrap control, with links to both documents, whether in the app or on the website. Each booking is acknowledged separately: the screen before payment names the transportation provider and the reservation details, and continuing to payment acknowledges them. The same acceptance and acknowledgment steps apply on every platform.

Drivers join by invitation. The Driver App Terms must be scrolled to the end before the accept control turns on, and you then accept by an affirmative action. You can save a copy of the agreement.

RVIP does not currently obtain background reports about drivers or riders. If it ever adopts a screening process, the required disclosure and authorization will be presented separately from these terms.

4 Signing for a business

If you sign for a carrier or other business, you confirm that you are authorized to bind it, and we record the business's legal name and your name and title. An invitation code or uploaded file is not itself a signed agreement.

5 What we keep

For each acceptance we keep the document name and version, the exact text or an unchangeable copy, the date and time, how you accepted, your account and any related reservation or business, the platform used (app or website), and the consent event. We collect device or network details only where lawful and needed. We protect these records from alteration, keep a record of corrections, never overwrite an earlier accepted version, and give you an accurate copy on request.

6 Retention and amendments

We keep these records for the periods described in the Privacy Policy and as the law requires, and longer during a legal hold. Material changes follow the notice and acceptance rules of the agreement involved; continued use does not replace your affirmative consent where the law or the agreement requires it. We keep prior versions with their dates.

7 Governing principles

This policy is administered consistently with the federal E-SIGN Act (15 U.S.C. §7001 et seq.), the Uniform Electronic Transactions Act as adopted in New Hampshire (RSA chapter 294-E) and in the state where you live, and other applicable law. It does not convert a record excluded from electronic treatment into a valid electronic instrument or waive nonwaivable consumer protections.
