Delete Your Account

RVIP Rider & RVIP Driver  ·  Seacoast RVIP

In short: You can request deletion of your RVIP Rider account, your RVIP Driver account, or both. You can delete your account from inside the app, or request it by email — no website sign-in required.
Section 01
Which accounts this covers

This page covers the two Seacoast RVIP mobile apps:

  • RVIP Rider — the passenger app used to book and manage rides.
  • RVIP Driver — the driver app used to accept and complete trips.

You can request deletion of either account, or both. Tell us which one applies when you contact us.

Section 02
Delete your account inside the app

The fastest way to delete your account is directly in the app:

  1. Open RVIP Rider or RVIP Driver and sign in.
  2. Go to the Profile screen.
  3. Tap Delete Account (listed under Sign Out) and confirm.

For your security, the app may ask you to sign in again before it can complete the deletion. If prompted, sign out and sign back in, then try again.

Section 03
Request deletion by email

If you cannot access the app, you can request deletion by email — you do not need to sign in to this website.

  1. Email info@seacoastrvip.com, preferably from the email address associated with your account.
  2. Use the subject RVIP account deletion request, and tell us which account to delete: Rider, Driver, or both.
  3. Include the account email address so we can locate your account. If you cannot access that email, let us know so we can discuss how to verify your request.
For your safety, never email your password, payment-card number, bank details, identity documents, or any sign-in verification codes. Sending an email begins a request; it does not automatically delete the account.

If the button does not open an email app, copy info@seacoastrvip.com into your preferred email service.

Section 04
What is deleted

When your deletion request is completed, we remove your ability to sign in and delete your account profile, including:

  • Your sign-in credentials and account access.
  • Your profile details (such as name, email, phone number) and profile photo.
  • Your saved preferences and any data stored locally on your device.

Your personal trip history is anonymized so it is no longer linked to you.

Section 05
What may be retained

Some records cannot be deleted immediately because we are required to keep them. We retain the following only for as long as required by applicable law and for legitimate business, tax, accounting, safety, and dispute-resolution purposes:

  • Trip and transaction records required for tax, accounting, and financial reporting.
  • Driver-qualification, consent, and safety records (for the RVIP Driver app).
  • Records subject to a legal hold or an open investigation, claim, or dispute.

Retained records are kept in a limited, de-identified form where possible and are not an active account. Deleting your account does not cancel any amount you lawfully owe and does not waive a refund you are owed.

Questions
