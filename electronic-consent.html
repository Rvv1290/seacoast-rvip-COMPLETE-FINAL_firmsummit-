Electronic Signature Policy & Consent

Seacoast RVIP LLC  ·  Effective October 31, 2026

Issued by Seacoast RVIP LLC, a New Hampshire limited liability company based in Hampton, NH. See all legal documents.

This policy governs electronic delivery, acknowledgment and signature of RVIP records. Seacoast RVIP LLC preserves the exact record accepted, the identity and authority of the signer, the method of assent and the time of the transaction. Authentication alone is not acceptance of every document.

Section 1
Consent to electronic records

By affirmatively accepting electronic delivery after being given access to this notice, you consent to receive and sign electronically the agreements, disclosures, notices and transaction records identified in the applicable workflow. Where law requires consumer consent under the E-SIGN Act, RVIP obtains that consent in a manner that reasonably demonstrates your ability to access the electronic form to be used. Merely displaying a notice is not sufficient when affirmative consent is required.

You need an internet-connected device, a current supported browser or supported RVIP app, a functioning email address, and software capable of opening and saving the provided document format, including PDF when used. You may save or print records for your files. RVIP provides notice of a material technical change creating a risk that you cannot access or retain future records and obtains renewed consent when required.

Section 2
Paper copies and withdrawal

You may request a paper copy or withdraw electronic-delivery consent by contacting info@seacoastrvip.com. RVIP does not charge for an ordinary copy request under this policy. Withdrawal applies prospectively and does not invalidate a prior lawful signature or an obligation already incurred. Some app-based services may not be available without necessary electronic records; RVIP explains the consequence and any available alternative rather than treating withdrawal as a waiver of legal rights. Keep contact information current through your account or support.

Section 3
Separate acts of assent

Rider account terms require the applicable affirmative acceptance with accessible Terms of Service and Privacy Policy links. Booking acknowledgment is recorded separately. The provider-information card uses a clear statement immediately above Continue to payment, explaining that continuing acknowledges the named transportation provider and reservation details; it does not require a second checkbox. Store the provider and reservation information presented with that acknowledgment.

Driver account creation uses an invitation and verified account information. Before continuing, the driver affirmatively accepts the specified conditions and terms. Acceptance of the driver agreement remains disabled until the agreement's end has been reached in the reading interface, followed by the required affirmative action. Reaching the end is an access control, not proof of comprehension. Give the driver an opportunity to save the agreement.

RVIP does not currently obtain background reports about drivers or riders. If it ever adopts a screening process, the required disclosure and authorization will be presented separately from these terms.

Section 4
Business authority and signatures

For a carrier or other business, record the legal entity, signer's name, title and authority. Complete the commercial and insurance schedules before signature. The counterparty and RVIP sign their respective blocks. A member signing a vehicle lease personally as owner and for the Company signs separately in each capacity. An invitation code or uploaded file is not itself an executed agreement.

Section 5
Evidence and integrity

The record must identify the signer or authenticated account, document name and version, exact accepted text or an immutable copy, date and time, acceptance method, relevant reservation or entity identifier and the consent event. Record device or network evidence only as lawful and necessary. Protect records against unauthorized alteration, preserve an audit trail of corrections and make an accurate copy available to the signer.

An implementation is not approved merely because it contains a checkbox. The release owner must test the actual links, consent states, record storage, version retrieval and copy-delivery functions before relying on them. Do not overwrite a historical accepted document with a new version.

Section 6
Retention and amendments

Follow the retention schedule in the Compliance Program Manual and applicable law. Preserve relevant records during a legal hold. Material amendments follow the notice and acceptance provisions of the underlying agreement; continued use does not replace affirmative consent when law or the agreement requires it. Maintain prior versions with their effective dates.

Section 7
Governing principles

This policy is administered consistently with 15 U.S.C. § 7001, New Hampshire RSA chapter 294-E, Massachusetts General Laws chapter 110G, Maine's Uniform Electronic Transactions Act and other applicable law. It does not convert a record excluded from electronic treatment into a valid electronic instrument or waive nonwaivable consumer protections.
