App Privacy Policy

RVIP Rider & RVIP Driver  ·  Seacoast RVIP LLC  ·  Effective October 31, 2026

This policy covers the RVIP Rider and RVIP Driver mobile apps and the RVIP booking, transportation and carrier-management platform behind them. It applies to riders, drivers, driver applicants, carrier representatives and anyone who contacts us through the apps. Seacoast RVIP LLC is a New Hampshire limited liability company and is responsible for administering this policy.

Booking on seacoastrvip.com instead? That is covered by the separate Website Privacy Policy.

Section 1
Information we collect

Account and contact information — your name, email address, telephone number, account identifiers and authentication records. Riders may also provide saved places, trip instructions and business-booking information.

Reservations generate pickup and destination addresses, requested times, route information, fares, payment status, cancellations and receipts. You may give us a flight number for an airport reservation.

Driver and carrier onboarding may include identity and licensing information, both sides of a driver's licence with issue and expiration dates, business ownership and tax documentation, vehicle details, registration, insurance and inspection records, and eligibility results. We do not obtain background reports about drivers or riders. Employment administration may require payroll, tax, work-authorization, timekeeping and benefits records.

Location. We collect trip-related location information when needed to provide the service and, with the required device permissions, precise device location. Driver location is used for authorized availability and active transportation functions. Where an employee driver saves a home base and uses the optional "Return to base" control after a completed trip, we record that address and the estimated road distance of each return leg for mileage, vehicle and tax records — the driver's location at the start of the leg is used only to measure that distance, and the route driven is not tracked. For hourly employees we also keep work-time records (clock-in, break and clock-out times, with no location) and hourly pay figures.

Communications and device data — support messages, in-app trip messages between riders and drivers, trip communications, feedback, consent and agreement records, device and application information, security events and notification identifiers.

Payments. Our payment providers process payment-card and bank information. We receive transaction identifiers, payment status and the limited payment details needed for receipts, refunds, reconciliation and support. Please do not send full card numbers, passwords or complete identity documents through ordinary support messages.

Section 2
Purposes and permissions

We use information to establish and secure accounts; assess onboarding eligibility; arrange, dispatch and perform transportation; identify the actual provider of a trip; collect payments and issue refunds; deliver receipts and service notices; investigate complaints and safety incidents; administer employment and carrier relationships; meet legal obligations; and protect against fraud and misuse.

We request consent where the law requires it, including for applicable sensitive-data processing and for optional marketing. A device permission is not a substitute for a separate consent required by law.

If you use Face ID, Touch ID or a device passcode with the app, that check happens on your device — we never receive or store your biometric data.

Denying location access may limit location-dependent features; you can enter trip addresses manually where that feature is available. Withdrawing a permission does not erase location already lawfully retained for a completed trip, a dispute or a legal obligation.

Section 3
Who receives information

The carrier and assigned driver receive the information reasonably needed to perform the reservation, including pickup and destination details and relevant contact or accessibility instructions. Riders receive the provider identity and the necessary driver and vehicle information. We do not disclose a driver's identity documents to a rider.

Authorized service providers support hosting and authentication, maps and routing, payments, email, notifications and flight information. The service uses:

  • Google Firebase and cloud services — hosting, authentication, database, Crashlytics, Analytics and App Check
  • Stripe — payment processing
  • Mapbox, Apple MapKit and Google geocoding — maps, routing and address lookup
  • Twilio — masked calling
  • A Render-hosted chat relay — in-trip messaging
  • FlightAware, AeroDataBox and ADS-B Exchange — flight information
  • Apple and Google account services — supported rider sign-in

Provider access is limited by the service, the applicable agreements and the law. Some providers independently process information for their own legally required functions, as described in their own notices, so we do not make a general promise that every recipient acts only as our processor.

Information may also be disclosed to professional advisers, insurers, regulators, law enforcement or courts where legally required or appropriately necessary to protect rights and safety. In a business transaction, information may transfer subject to applicable confidentiality, notice and privacy obligations.

We do not sell personal information or authorize its use for targeted advertising. A material change to that practice would require the notices, choices and consents the law demands. Note that ordinary trip communications may reveal your telephone number to the recipient — please do not assume calls or messages are anonymous or masked.
Section 4
Retention and deletion

We keep information for the period reasonably necessary for its purpose and for applicable recordkeeping, safety, accounting, insurance and employment obligations, and for the establishment or defence of legal claims. Different records have different periods, and transaction and contractual records may be retained after an account closes. Information subject to a legal hold is not deleted while the hold applies.

Our internal baseline periods are:

Record typeBaseline retention
Trip, payment, refund, receipt, provider-display and reservation-acceptance records7 years
Driver, vehicle and carrier qualification evidenceRelationship + 3 years
In-app trip messagesThe trip, then up to 24 months for support, safety and dispute resolution
Governing records, IP assignments, adopted policy versions and signed organizational decisionsPermanent
Precise locationMinimized, and reviewed for deletion or de-identification once no longer needed

Legal holds and tax, insurance, safety, fraud, complaint, payment, employment and litigation needs may require longer retention.

Closing an account does not immediately erase every record. Deletion can involve removing identifiers, deleting active records, and allowing protected backups to expire through their normal cycle. We restrict retained information to lawful purposes. We do not promise deletion from every backup on a fixed date, and we do not claim that all deletion is automatic. Eligible profile data is not considered for deletion until at least ninety (90) days after a closure request, following a documented retention review confirming that no legal hold applies. Records we are required to keep — trip history, payment records, driver-qualification records, consent records and safety records — are retained regardless of account closure.

See Delete Your Account for how to make a deletion request.

Section 5
Requesting your information

Use Request My Data in the app's Privacy Policy section, or contact us at info@seacoastrvip.com with "Privacy request" in the subject. You may request access to, a copy of, correction of or deletion of your information, and exercise applicable consent or processing choices. You may also request account deletion from a web browser, without the app, at seacoastrvip.com/delete-account.

We verify identity in a manner proportionate to the request and the sensitivity of the information, and we may ask for additional information or proof of an authorized representative's authority. Never send us a password or a complete payment-card number.

When an export is ready we provide instructions for secure delivery, or a Download My Data control where available. A completed export may be delivered as an encrypted ZIP containing a PDF guide, CSV files for readable tabular records and JSON for structured portability where feasible, using an authenticated link that expires. We record delivery and download events and may require renewed authentication before issuing another link. Exports exclude other people's protected information, security secrets and material the law permits or requires us to withhold. Availability of a download does not mean an account-deletion request has been completed.

New Hampshire Data Privacy Act. Where the Act applies, we respond within 45 days, with a permitted additional 45 days where reasonably necessary and explained within the initial period. You may appeal a refusal through the same contact channel; we respond to an appeal within 60 days and, if it is denied, explain how to contact the New Hampshire Attorney General. Other applicable legal deadlines control where they differ.

Some statutory consumer rights do not apply to employment or business-contact information, or to businesses below statutory thresholds. We nevertheless accept requests and evaluate them under the laws and obligations applicable to the record. Requests are ordinarily free; any legally permitted fee, or a refusal for excessive requests, will be explained before charging. We explain any lawful refusal or limitation, and we do not retaliate against you for exercising a privacy right.

Section 6
Security and incident handling

We maintain administrative, technical and physical safeguards appropriate to the information and the risk, including access controls, restricted document handling and incident-response procedures.

No service can guarantee absolute security. We provide legally required breach notices when the applicable conditions are met. This policy does not represent that any particular vendor eliminates all security risk.

Section 7
Adults and communications

Our service is for adults aged 18 or older and does not transport minors under our current policy. We do not knowingly establish accounts for children. Contact us if you believe a child has supplied information so that we can investigate and take appropriate action.

Necessary service communications are distinct from marketing. Optional marketing consent is not a condition of buying transportation, and you may use the stated unsubscribe or opt-out method at any time. Revoking marketing consent does not stop receipts, safety notices or other necessary lawful service communications.

Section 8
Changes and contact

We publish the effective date above and provide notice of material changes as required by law. A new policy does not retroactively authorize incompatible uses that require consent.

Questions and privacy requests may be sent to info@seacoastrvip.com, identifying "Privacy request" in the subject line.
