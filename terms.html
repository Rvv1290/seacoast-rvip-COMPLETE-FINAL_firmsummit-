Section 01
Who we are and what these Terms cover

Seacoast RVIP, LLC ("RVIP," "we," "us") is a New Hampshire limited liability company based in Hampton, New Hampshire. These Terms cover your use of seacoastrvip.com and reservations made through the website, by email or by phone. Rides booked in the RVIP Rider app are governed by the Rider Terms of Service and the policies it lists; for an app booking, those app documents control.

Section 02
Our service

We provide pre-arranged, reservation-only private transportation. We do not offer on-demand or street-hail rides. We offer trips within New Hampshire and pre-arranged trips between states, where the carrier performing the trip holds the required authority, insurance and airport permissions. We do not offer trips that start and end in Massachusetts, and trips that start and end in Maine are not currently offered. Not every route is available; we confirm availability before your reservation is final.

Section 03
Eligibility

Our service is for adults 18 and older. We do not transport minors, even when accompanied.

Section 04
Reservations

A reservation is confirmed only when we confirm it to you in writing by email or text. Please give accurate pickup, destination, time, passenger, luggage and flight details. Changes depend on availability; if a change affects the price, we tell you the new price before the change is confirmed.

Section 05
Prices and payment

We quote a flat fare before you book. The quote includes tolls and applicable airport fees, and any sales tax that applies is shown within the total. We do not use surge pricing. Extra charges apply only for an additional service you request, such as an extra stop or added waiting time, and we tell you the price or how it is calculated before we provide it. Tips are optional. Payment is made through the payment methods we offer when you book. Never send card numbers or passwords by email.

Section 06
Cancellations and refunds

You may cancel before pickup without a cancellation fee, and we refund any amount you prepaid, including any tip. We do not currently charge a no-show fee. Any future cancellation or no-show fee will apply only to reservations made after we clearly disclose it. If we cancel and you do not accept a replacement, you receive a full refund. If a ride is cut short, we review the service performed and the reason, and refund what the law requires. Refunds go to your original payment method; your bank controls when they post.

Section 07
Passenger conduct and safety

Please follow your driver's reasonable safety instructions and the law. The number of passengers may not exceed the vehicle's seating capacity, and everyone must use a seat belt. No smoking or vaping, no illegal substances and no hazardous materials; open alcohol only where the law allows. Harassment, threats, violence or discrimination toward anyone are not allowed. A driver may end a ride for a real safety concern; any refund is decided under Section 6. In an emergency, call 911.

Section 08
Accessibility and service animals

We do not discriminate on the basis of disability. Service animals ride at no extra charge, and drivers help with folding wheelchairs and walkers that fit safely in the vehicle, at no charge. Tell us about accessibility needs when you book. Our Safety, Accessibility, Nondiscrimination and Zero-Tolerance Policy has the details.

Section 09
Damage and lost items

If you or your passengers damage a vehicle beyond normal wear, we may ask you to pay the reasonable, documented cost to repair or clean it. We will show you the evidence and give you at least seven days to respond before resolving a disputed claim. We will try to help you recover items left in a vehicle, but we cannot guarantee recovery.

Section 10
Delays

We plan for traffic and flight changes, but we are not responsible for delays caused by events beyond our reasonable control, such as severe weather, road closures, accidents or airport disruptions. If we cannot perform your trip for such a reason, you receive a full refund.
