You enter your pickup, destination, any stops, the pickup date and time, the vehicle class, the service lane, and — for airport Trips — an optional flight number. You may add special requests. It includes the traffic normally expected at that time and RVIP's published service-hours rate for late-night and early-morning pickups, and it never responds to real-time demand. If you change the pickup time, the app or website shows the updated all-in price before you book. The Pricing Policy explains how the price is calculated.

Massachusetts pickups and drop-offs, including at Boston Logan International Airport, are offered only for trips between Massachusetts and another state; a stop in Massachusetts along the way is permitted. Trips within New Hampshire are provided by RVIP's Own Lane. Trips that start and end in Maine are not currently offered. The app or website will not book a trip outside these limits; if one is booked in error, RVIP cancels it and refunds any payment in full. A partner's availability depends on that partner's authority and insurance.

Reserved hours and the booked reserved-time rate or waiting charge remain unchanged; only the affected driving component is recalculated, and the reservation keeps its agreed held-service or return structure.
