1.1 The parties

These Rider Terms of Service are a contract between you and Seacoast RVIP, LLC, a New Hampshire limited liability company (RVIP, we or us). Contact us at info@seacoastrvip.com.

These Terms govern your use of the RVIP rider app, the website at seacoastrvip.com, and the booking, payment and support services we provide through them (together, the "Services"). A "Trip" is a ride you book through the Services.

1.2 What RVIP is

RVIP is a pre-arranged, reservation-based premium ground-transportation service. You book in the app before pickup — as soon as possible, or scheduled up to 30 days ahead.

  • There are no street hails. Drivers may not pick up anyone who has not booked through the app.
  • There is no personal-vehicle ridesharing. Every Trip is performed by a licensed carrier in a commercially insured for-hire vehicle with a professional driver. No Trip on RVIP is performed in a personal vehicle.
  • There is no surge pricing. The price you are quoted before you book is the price.

Section 4
How Booking Works
4.1 What you enter

You enter your pickup, destination, any stops, the date and time (or "as soon as possible"), the vehicle class, the service lane, and — for airport Trips — an optional flight number. You may add special requests. You must be truthful about the number of passengers and the amount of luggage. The vehicle's capacity is shown before you book and may not be exceeded.

4.2 Your all-in quoted price

Before you book, the app shows your all-in quoted price. It includes every mandatory charge, such as any airport fee that applies. There is no surge pricing, ever. The quoted price is the price you pay, whether the Trip is an Own-Lane Trip or a Partner Trip. An optional tip is chosen at payment. The Pricing Policy explains how the price is calculated.

4.3 Scheduling and the Radar

When you select Schedule a ride, RVIP records your requested reservation. The request is considered first for the eligible own operation and management, which may offer it to an approved partner carrier or its authorized driver. Eligibility depends on the actual carrier, driver, vehicle, territory, insurance and applicable airport permissions.

RVIP may dispatch its own employees. A partner carrier controls its personnel and may accept or decline an offer. No particular provider is guaranteed until acceptance and identification in the reservation.

4.4 Provider selection

Riders do not select a preferred individual driver. Management offers the reservation to eligible providers, and the accepting provider is identified before payment.

4.5 Acceptance

When a driver accepts your Trip, the app shows you the carrier, the driver and the vehicle. You then review and pay (Sections 5 and 6).

4.6 If no carrier accepts

You are not charged unless a carrier accepts and you complete payment. A scheduling request alone does not guarantee provider availability. If no carrier accepts, you may cancel without charge and contact support for assistance.

4.7 Where Trips are available

Availability depends on the approved territory of the actual carrier. Seacoast RVIP, LLC may provide eligible New Hampshire intrastate and approved interstate service once its own required credentials are in place. Its Own Lane does not perform Massachusetts-to-Massachusetts trips. A partner's availability depends on that partner's authority and insurance.

Section 5

5.1 Own-Lane Trips

For an Own-Lane Trip, Seacoast RVIP, LLC is the carrier and is responsible for the transportation. It uses Company-operated, commercially insured vehicles and authorized drivers.

5.2 Partner Trips

For a Partner Trip, the Partner Carrier named in the app is the carrier of record and bears primary liability for the transportation services. Each Partner Carrier is an independent company that holds its own operating authority, vehicles, insurance and drivers. RVIP's agreement with each Partner Carrier requires commercial auto liability insurance covering for-hire passenger transportation with a combined single limit of at least $1,500,000.

5.3 Provider information

The reservation identifies whether Seacoast RVIP, LLC or a named partner carrier provides the transportation. RVIP arranges the booking, administers payment and provides support in either case.

A. At booking:

At booking, you are told that RVIP or an approved partner may provide the ride and that you will see the carrier, driver and vehicle before paying.

B. On the Schedule screen:

The reservation displays the actual accepting provider when assigned, not an unassigned carrier placeholder after acceptance.

C. Partner Trip disclosure

Transportation provider: the accepting partner's legal name. Payment is administered by Seacoast RVIP, LLC for the arranged reservation.

D. Own-Lane Trip disclosure:

Transportation provider: Seacoast RVIP, LLC.

E. Before a carrier has accepted:

Until acceptance, the reservation states that the provider has not yet been assigned; it does not falsely identify a carrier.

5.4 The acknowledgment before payment

After a carrier accepts, you tap Pay. A Reservation details screen then shows:

  • the carrier's legal name;
  • the identification of that company as the transportation provider;
  • your driver's name; and
  • the vehicle and its licence plate, with the reminder: "Only enter the vehicle that matches this plate."

Immediately above Continue to payment, the card states: By continuing, you acknowledge the named provider as your transportation provider and agree to the reservation details. Selecting that button records the acknowledgment; there is no second checkbox on this card.

We keep a record of your acknowledgment: your account, the Trip, the carrier, the lane, the exact disclosure text, its version, the app version, the server time and how you acknowledged. We keep it for the life of your account plus 7 years. If the carrier or the wording changes before your Trip, you must acknowledge again before payment. The receipt for each Trip also names the carrier of record.

It is responsible for its driver, its vehicle, its insurance and its compliance with the transportation laws that apply to it, and it bears primary liability for the transportation services. Claims about the transportation itself (for example, a vehicle accident) are primarily the Partner Carrier's responsibility and its insurer's.
  • On an Own-Lane Trip, Seacoast RVIP, LLC is the carrier and is responsible for the transportation.

Section 6
Payment, Tips and Receipts
6.1 When you pay

You pay after provider acceptance and the reservation acknowledgment. Payment is marked paid only after server confirmation. The driver may arrive before you pay, but cannot start the Trip until payment is confirmed. If payment fails, retry through the authorized flow or contact support; acceptance alone is not payment.

6.2 Stripe

Stripe processes card payments and provides transaction information needed for receipts, refunds and support. Do not send complete card details to RVIP support. Payment received by RVIP through its authorized collection channel discharges the corresponding amount owed for the Trip; you do not bear a failure-to-remit risk between RVIP and a partner carrier. The payment provider's applicable terms and privacy notice also apply.

6.3 Price shown is the price

You pay the disclosed total, less an applicable agreed promotion, plus any optional tip. A rider-requested change or additional service requires advance disclosure and agreement to its price. A damage claim is handled under Section 8.3 and is not an undisclosed automatic charge.

6.4 Tips

Tips are optional. 100% of your tip is passed through. On a Partner Trip, the tip is paid to the Partner Carrier, which must pass it in full to the driver. On an Own-Lane Trip, the tip goes to the driver who drove you. RVIP keeps no part of any tip. RVIP's platform fee (Section 6.5) is not a tip and is not a gratuity for the driver.

6.5 How the fare is shared (transparency)

On a Partner Trip, RVIP keeps a platform fee of 18% of the fare (tips excluded). The Partner Carrier receives 82% of the fare plus 100% of tips, paid to the carrier company through Stripe. The platform fee comes out of the fare; it is not an extra charge to you. On an Own-Lane Trip, the fare is RVIP's revenue and the tip goes to the driver.

6.6 Promotions

Promotions are subject to the conditions shown with the offer. Any applicable discount appears in the quoted price before booking. Promotions have no cash value unless the offer expressly provides otherwise.

6.7 Receipts

The transaction record identifies the provider, trip, fare, tip, total and payment status. You may request a copy through support.

6.8 Billing questions and disputes

Contact info@seacoastrvip.com or in-app support promptly, preferably within 60 days of a charge. We aim to acknowledge ordinary billing concerns within five business days. This request does not shorten legal rights or card-provider dispute periods.

7.3 If a carrier cancels
