Driver App Terms

Seacoast RVIP, LLC  ·  Last updated: November 1, 2026

One set of terms and policies. The same documents govern whether you book in the RVIP Rider or RVIP Driver app or on seacoastrvip.com. See all legal documents.
SEACOAST RVIP, LLC
Company identity and operating model. Seacoast RVIP, LLC is a New Hampshire limited liability company owned by its sole member, Ramon Vargas Villa. Seacoast RVIP, LLC owns and operates the RVIP digital platform, including the RVIP Rider App, RVIP Driver App, and RVIP Operations and Compliance Center. The platform supports reservation intake, dispatch coordination, disclosed pricing, payment administration, trip communications, customer support, and compliance records. For an Own-Lane Trip, Seacoast RVIP, LLC is the transportation provider and carrier of record and uses its authorized drivers and company-operated vehicles. Initially, a vehicle may remain titled personally to Ramon Vargas Villa and be leased to Seacoast RVIP, LLC under Document 10; as the business grows, vehicles may instead be titled directly to Seacoast RVIP, LLC. In the Partner Lane, the approved partner transportation company that accepts the reservation is the transportation provider and carrier of record. Ownership and operation of the apps does not make Seacoast RVIP, LLC the carrier for a Partner-Lane Trip or transfer a carrier's legal duties.

These Terms govern Driver app access. They do not replace an employment agreement or your carrier's duties. Document 09 governs Own-Lane employment. Partner drivers work for their carrier. The linked Privacy and Safety Policies apply.

Section 1
Invitation account and eligibility

Signup requires a single-use invitation tied to your carrier and identity. Create your own account, verify email and keep contact information accurate. Never share an account or permit a substitute driver. Leaving a carrier ends access under it; a new affiliation requires authorization. You must be at least 21 or the insurer's higher minimum, licensed, carrier-authorized, commercially covered and lawfully screened, with applicable driver and airport credentials. Email verification allows onboarding to continue, not passenger service.

Section 2
Documents and vehicles

Upload license front and back and its dates where present. Then enter vehicle details and commercial coverage for the vehicle and you, registration, plate evidence, safety inspection and applicable airport credentials. Each of up to three vehicles is approved separately. Profile and onboarding status refer to the same records. Use only the selected approved vehicle shown for the trip, never a personal vehicle or personal auto policy.

Renew documents early. A lapse restricts the affected driver, vehicle, company or airport until verified renewal; unrelated manual holds remain. Report lost license, coverage or authority immediately and stop affected service. Report other material changes within five days. False documents or identity substitution are prohibited.

Section 3
Screening

Receive a separate stand-alone disclosure and sign authorization before a Checkr report is ordered under Document 08. RVIP decides platform qualification and its own employment decisions; a partner decides its employment matters. Report-based adverse decisions require notice, a report copy, rights summary, response opportunity and final notice where applicable. State and airport clearances remain separate.

Section 4
Trip offers

Reservations go first to the eligible own operation and management. Management may then offer a trip to approved partners or their authorized drivers. Partners may decline or ignore offers without penalty, ranking loss or quotas; no volume or earnings is guaranteed. Your partner controls your hours and pay. RVIP employees follow Document 09 schedules and lawful assignments, with paid work time and the right to refuse unsafe or unlawful work. No preferred-driver selection is promised to riders.

Accept only trips your carrier and vehicle can lawfully perform. Acceptance identifies carrier, driver and vehicle. Cancel accepted trips early if performance becomes impossible. RVIP may address repeated failures of accepted service with carrier management; declining an offer is different.

Section 5
Payment and earnings

Do not start until the app shows server-confirmed payment. Arrival does not remove this requirement. Contact support for pending payment; do not change payment status yourself, collect cash or demand extra charges. Partner earnings displays are informational trip records, not an RVIP paycheck. RVIP pays the carrier through Stripe Connect; it pays you and must pass through all tips. Own-Lane employees receive hourly wages, overtime and tips through payroll, not the partner fare split.

Section 6
Safety conduct and data

Follow Document 14. Never drive impaired, fatigued or with a safety defect. No discrimination, harassment, service-animal refusal, disability surcharge, street hail, solicitation, unauthorized substitution or off-app cash trip. Confirm identity, plate, capacity and seat belts; use the phone safely. Service is adults only. Call 911 in emergencies and report incidents as soon as safe.

Cabin recording is not authorized. Do not retaliate for protected reports or restrict lawful discussion of wages and working conditions. Use rider information only for the trip and authorized safety, support or legal purposes. Do not save contacts, market to riders or contact them after trips except through the approved lost-item process. Ordinary phone calls are not promised to conceal numbers.

The app uses permitted location while online or performing trips for dispatch, navigation, tracking and safety. Location access is required for online service. The Privacy Policy governs retention, access and deletion requests. Use a locked updated device and report compromise. You may request your data or account deletion in the app or at info@seacoastrvip.com; required records and legal holds may remain.

Section 7
App rights and restrictions

RVIP grants a personal, limited, nonexclusive, nontransferable license for approved service. Do not scrape, spoof location, bypass security, introduce malware or reverse-engineer except as law permits. RVIP retains its software and branding. Partner personnel shall not falsely claim to be RVIP employees; actual employees may identify their employer.

Section 8
Restrictions and review

RVIP may restrict access for lost qualifications, expired evidence, carrier termination, credible safety reports, fraud, data misuse, law or material breach. Reasons and an opportunity to respond will be provided unless law or safety prevents disclosure. A protective restriction is not a final finding. Document 08 applies to report-based decisions. Partners manage employment consequences; RVIP manages its own employees lawfully. You may stop using the app and request closure.

Section 9
Liability disputes and changes

The app is available without a guarantee of uninterrupted service or trip volume. To the extent lawful, RVIP is not liable for indirect loss caused solely by unavailability. Nothing limits wages, workers' compensation, death or injury caused by negligence, gross negligence, fraud, willful misconduct or non-waivable rights.

Contact info@seacoastrvip.com about concerns. NH law applies without removing mandatory protections elsewhere. Disputes may be heard in Rockingham County state courts or the United States District Court for the District of New Hampshire where jurisdiction exists, subject to legally required forums and small-claims rights. These Terms impose no mandatory arbitration. Material changes require notice and acceptance before further trips. Document 09 controls employment. Unenforceable terms do not invalidate the remainder. Electronic assent follows Document 16.
