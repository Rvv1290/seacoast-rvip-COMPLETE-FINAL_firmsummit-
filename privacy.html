Privacy Policy

Seacoast RVIP, LLC  ·  Last updated: November 1, 2026

One set of terms and policies. The same documents govern whether you book in the RVIP Rider or RVIP Driver app or on seacoastrvip.com. See all legal documents.
SEACOAST RVIP, LLC
Company identity and operating model. Seacoast RVIP, LLC is a New Hampshire limited liability company owned by its sole member, Ramon Vargas Villa. Seacoast RVIP, LLC owns and operates the RVIP digital platform, including the RVIP Rider App, RVIP Driver App, and RVIP Operations and Compliance Center. The platform supports reservation intake, dispatch coordination, disclosed pricing, payment administration, trip communications, customer support, and compliance records. For an Own-Lane Trip, Seacoast RVIP, LLC is the transportation provider and carrier of record and uses its authorized drivers and company-operated vehicles. Initially, a vehicle may remain titled personally to Ramon Vargas Villa and be leased to Seacoast RVIP, LLC under Document 10; as the business grows, vehicles may instead be titled directly to Seacoast RVIP, LLC. In the Partner Lane, the approved partner transportation company that accepts the reservation is the transportation provider and carrier of record. Ownership and operation of the apps does not make Seacoast RVIP, LLC the carrier for a Partner-Lane Trip or transfer a carrier's legal duties.

Seacoast RVIP, LLC operates RVIP booking, transportation and carrier-management services. This policy explains how we collect, use, disclose and retain personal information, and how you can make a privacy request. It applies to riders, applicants, drivers, carrier representatives and people contacting RVIP. Employment records receive the additional protections applicable to the employment relationship.

Effective date: November 1, 2026  ·  Last updated: November 1, 2026
Section 1
Information we collect

Account and contact information includes your name, email address, telephone number, account identifiers and authentication records. Riders may provide saved places, trip instructions and business-booking information. Reservations generate pickup and destination addresses, requested times, route information, fares, payment status, cancellations and receipts.

Driver and carrier onboarding may include identity and licensing information, both sides of a driver's license, issue and expiration dates, business ownership and tax documentation, vehicle details, registration, insurance and inspection records, screening authorizations and eligibility results. Background reports are handled through the screening process and applicable restrictions; they are not shared with riders. Employment administration may require payroll, tax, work-authorization, timekeeping and benefits records.

We collect trip-related location information when needed to provide the service and, with the required permissions, precise device location. Driver location is used for authorized availability and active transportation functions. We also process support messages, trip communications, feedback, consent and agreement records, device and application information, security events and notification identifiers. You may give us flight information for an airport reservation.

Payment providers process payment-card and bank information. RVIP receives transaction identifiers, payment status and limited payment details needed for receipts, refunds, reconciliation and support. Do not send full card numbers, passwords or complete identity documents through ordinary support messages.

Section 2
Purposes and permissions

We use information to establish and secure accounts; assess onboarding eligibility; arrange, dispatch and perform transportation; identify the actual provider; collect payments and issue refunds; deliver receipts and service notices; investigate complaints and safety incidents; administer employment and carrier relationships; meet legal obligations; and protect against fraud and misuse.

We request consent when law requires it, including applicable sensitive-data processing and optional marketing. A device permission does not replace a separate consent required by law. Denying location access may limit location-dependent features; you can enter trip addresses manually where that feature is available. Withdrawing permission does not erase location already lawfully retained for a completed trip, dispute or legal obligation.

Section 3
Who receives information

The carrier and assigned driver receive information reasonably needed to perform the reservation, including pickup and destination details and relevant contact or accessibility instructions. Riders receive the provider identity and necessary driver and vehicle information. We do not disclose a driver's identity documents or background report to a rider.

Authorized service providers support hosting and authentication, maps and routing, payments, screening, email, notifications and flight information. The service may use Google Firebase and cloud services, Stripe, Mapbox, Checkr, SendGrid, Apple or Google account services for supported rider login, and configured flight-data providers. Driver account creation uses the invitation process. Provider access is limited by the service, applicable agreements and law; some providers independently process information for their own legally required functions, as described in their notices.

Information may also be disclosed to professional advisers, insurers, regulators, law enforcement or courts when legally required or appropriately necessary to protect rights and safety. In a business transaction, information may transfer subject to applicable confidentiality, notice and privacy obligations. We do not make a general promise that every recipient acts only as our processor.

RVIP does not sell personal information or authorize its use for targeted advertising under this policy. A material change to that practice requires the notices, choices and consents required by applicable law. Ordinary trip communications may reveal your telephone number to the recipient; do not assume calls or messages are anonymous or masked.

Section 4
Retention and deletion

We keep information for the period reasonably necessary for its purpose, applicable recordkeeping requirements, safety, accounting, insurance, employment obligations and the establishment or defense of legal claims. Different records have different periods. Transaction and contractual records may be retained after an account closes. Information subject to a legal hold is not deleted while the hold applies.

Closing an account does not immediately erase every trip, payment, consent, screening or employment record. Deletion can involve removing identifiers, deleting active records and allowing protected backups to expire through their normal cycle. We restrict retained information to lawful purposes. We do not promise deletion from every backup on a fixed date or claim that all deletion is automatic.

Section 5
Requesting your information

Use Request my data in the app's Privacy Policy section, or contact RVIP at info@seacoastrvip.com. You may request access, a copy, correction or deletion of information, and applicable consent or processing choices. We verify identity in a manner proportionate to the request and the sensitivity of the information. We may request additional information or proof of an authorized representative's authority. Do not send a password or complete payment-card number.

When an export is ready, we provide instructions for secure delivery or a Download data control where available. A readable summary and structured files may be used together. Exports exclude other people's protected information, security secrets and material that law permits or requires us to withhold. A request does not itself authorize release to an unverified recipient. Access links may expire for security; contact support if a new link is needed.

Where the New Hampshire Data Privacy Act applies, we respond within 45 days, with a permitted additional 45 days when reasonably necessary and explained within the initial period. You may appeal a refusal through the same contact channel; we respond to an appeal within 60 days and explain how to contact the New Hampshire Attorney General if it is denied. Other applicable legal deadlines control when different. We explain a lawful refusal or limitation and do not retaliate for exercising a privacy right.

Some statutory consumer rights do not apply to employment or business-contact information or to businesses below statutory thresholds. RVIP nevertheless accepts requests and evaluates them under the laws and obligations applicable to the record. Requests ordinarily are free; any legally permitted fee or refusal for excessive requests will be explained before charging.

Section 6
Security and incident handling

We maintain administrative, technical and physical safeguards appropriate to the information and risk, including access controls, restricted document handling and incident-response procedures. No service can guarantee absolute security. We provide legally required breach notices when the applicable conditions are met. This policy does not represent that a particular vendor eliminates all security risk.

Section 7
Adults and communications

RVIP's service is for adults aged 18 or older and does not transport minors under its current policy. We do not knowingly establish accounts for children. Contact us if you believe a child supplied information so we can investigate and take appropriate action.

Necessary service communications are distinct from marketing. Optional marketing consent is not a condition of buying transportation, and you may use the stated unsubscribe or opt-out method. Revoking marketing consent does not stop receipts, safety notices or other necessary lawful service communications.

Section 8
Changes and contact

We publish the effective date and provide notice of material changes as required by law. A new policy does not retroactively authorize incompatible uses requiring consent. Questions and privacy requests may be sent to info@seacoastrvip.com, identifying Privacy request in the subject. Seacoast RVIP, LLC is responsible for administering this policy.
