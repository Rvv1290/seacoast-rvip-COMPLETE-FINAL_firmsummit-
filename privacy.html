Privacy Policy

SEACOAST RVIP, LLC
Company status. Seacoast RVIP, LLC is a New Hampshire limited liability company, formed on October 29, 2026 (New Hampshire Business ID 1045034), that owns and operates the RVIP Rider and RVIP Driver apps and the website at seacoastrvip.com.

Seacoast RVIP, LLC operates RVIP booking, transportation and carrier-management services through the RVIP Rider and RVIP Driver apps and the website at seacoastrvip.com (including any web booking or corporate booking portal). This policy explains how we collect, use, disclose and retain personal information, and how you can make a privacy request. It applies to riders, applicants, drivers, carrier representatives and people contacting RVIP. Employment records receive the additional protections applicable to the employment relationship.

Last updated: November 1, 2026. This version applies to you from when you accept it in the app or on the website. Website adoption clarification, 2026-11-04.

Platform consistency. This Privacy Policy is the same master text for the RVIP apps and the website at seacoastrvip.com. The version shown when you accept is the version in effect. Current versions are published in the apps and on seacoastrvip.com.

1 Information we collect

Account and contact information includes your name, email address, telephone number, account identifiers and authentication records. Riders may provide saved places, trip instructions and business-booking information. Reservations generate pickup and destination addresses, requested times, route information, fares, payment status, cancellations and receipts.

Driver and carrier onboarding may include identity and licensing information, both sides of a driver's license, issue and expiration dates, business ownership and tax documentation, vehicle details, registration, insurance and inspection records, and eligibility results. RVIP does not obtain background reports about drivers or riders. Employment administration may require payroll, tax, work-authorization, timekeeping and benefits records.

We collect trip-related location information when needed to provide the service and, with the required permissions, precise device location. Driver location is used for authorized availability and active transportation functions. When an RVIP Own-Lane employee driver saves a return address (home base) and uses the optional Return to base control after a completed Trip, we record that address and, for each return leg, its estimated road distance to the base, to keep the Company's mileage, vehicle and tax records; the driver's location when the leg starts is used only to measure that distance, and the route driven is not tracked. For RVIP Own-Lane hourly employees, we also keep work-time records — clock-in, break and clock-out times, with no location — and hourly pay figures, as employment and payroll records. We also process support messages, in-app trip messages between riders and drivers, trip communications, feedback, consent and agreement records, device and application information, security events and notification identifiers. You may give us flight information for an airport reservation.

Payment providers process payment-card and bank information. RVIP receives transaction identifiers, payment status and limited payment details needed for receipts, refunds, reconciliation and support. Do not send full card numbers, passwords or complete identity documents through ordinary support messages.

2 Purposes and permissions

We use information to establish and secure accounts; assess onboarding eligibility; arrange, dispatch and perform transportation; identify the actual provider; collect payments and issue refunds; deliver receipts and service notices; investigate complaints and safety incidents; administer employment and carrier relationships; meet legal obligations; and protect against fraud and misuse.

We request consent when law requires it, including applicable sensitive-data processing and optional marketing. A device permission does not replace a separate consent required by law. If you use Face ID, Touch ID or a device passcode with the app, that check happens on your device; RVIP does not receive or store your biometric data. Denying location access may limit location-dependent features; you can enter trip addresses manually where that feature is available. Withdrawing permission does not erase location already lawfully retained for a completed trip, dispute or legal obligation.

3 Who receives information

Before a driver accepts a Trip, the driver sees only the approximate pickup and drop-off area, the time and trip details such as passengers, luggage and vehicle type, and never the rider's name or contact details. After acceptance, the assigned driver and carrier receive what is needed to perform the Trip: the rider's first name and last initial, the exact pickup, stop and drop-off addresses, flight details for airport pickups, and the rider's trip and accessibility instructions. Drivers never receive a rider's phone number, email address, payment details or saved places. Riders receive the provider identity and the driver's first name, photo, rating and vehicle make, model, color and plate, and never the driver's phone number or home address. We do not disclose a driver's identity documents to a rider.

Authorized service providers support hosting and authentication, maps and routing, payments, email, notifications and flight information. The service may use Google Firebase and cloud services, Stripe, Mapbox, Twilio for masked calling, the Render-hosted chat relay, Apple MapKit and Google geocoding for address lookup, FlightAware, AeroDataBox and ADS-B Exchange for flight information, Apple or Google account services for supported rider login, and Firebase Crashlytics, Analytics and App Check. Driver account creation uses the invitation process. Provider access is limited by the service, applicable agreements and law; some providers independently process information for their own legally required functions, as described in their notices.

Information may also be disclosed to professional advisers, insurers, regulators, law enforcement or courts when legally required or appropriately necessary to protect rights and safety. In a business transaction, information may transfer subject to applicable confidentiality, notice and privacy obligations. We do not make a general promise that every recipient acts only as our processor.

RVIP does not sell personal information or authorize its use for targeted advertising under this policy. A material change to that practice requires the notices, choices and consents required by applicable law. Trip chat does not share phone numbers. Calls between riders and drivers are offered only through RVIP's private calling line, provided by Twilio, which hides both parties' real numbers; RVIP does not give either party the other's number. The private-line routing record (the two numbers and the trip) expires automatically 12 hours after it is opened. Authorized RVIP staff can access trip chat and call routing records for support, safety and legal purposes.

4 Retention and deletion

We keep information for the period reasonably necessary for its purpose, applicable recordkeeping requirements, safety, accounting, insurance, employment obligations and the establishment or defense of legal claims. Different records have different periods. Transaction and contractual records may be retained after an account closes. Information subject to a legal hold is not deleted while the hold applies. In-app trip messages are retained for the trip and up to 24 months for support, safety and dispute resolution, then deleted, subject to legal holds.

Closing an account does not immediately erase every trip, payment, consent or employment record. Deletion can involve removing identifiers, deleting active records and allowing protected backups to expire through their normal cycle. We restrict retained information to lawful purposes. We do not promise deletion from every backup on a fixed date or claim that all deletion is automatic. Eligible profile data is not considered for deletion until at least ninety (90) days after a closure request, following a documented retention review confirming no applicable legal hold. Records we are required to keep — including trip history, payment records, driver-qualification records, consent records and safety records — are retained regardless of account closure.

5 Requesting your information

Use Request my data in the app's Privacy Policy section, the equivalent control on the website where available, or contact RVIP at info@seacoastrvip.com. You may request access, a copy, correction or deletion of information, and applicable consent or processing choices. We verify identity in a manner proportionate to the request and the sensitivity of the information. We may request additional information or proof of an authorized representative's authority. Do not send a password or complete payment-card number. You may also request account deletion from a web browser without the app at https://www.seacoastrvip.com/delete-account.html; that page identifies both RVIP apps, explains the request, and distinguishes account closure from lawfully retained records.

When an export is ready, we provide instructions for secure delivery or a Download data control where available. A readable summary and structured files may be used together. Exports exclude other people's protected information, security secrets and material that law permits or requires us to withhold. A request does not itself authorize release to an unverified recipient. Access links may expire for security; contact support if a new link is needed.

Where the New Hampshire Data Privacy Act applies, we respond within 45 days, with a permitted additional 45 days when reasonably necessary and explained within the initial period. You may appeal a refusal through the same contact channel; we respond to an appeal within 60 days and explain how to contact the New Hampshire Attorney General if it is denied. Other applicable legal deadlines control when different. We explain a lawful refusal or limitation and do not retaliate for exercising a privacy right.

Your state's privacy rights. Depending on where you live, your state's privacy law may give you rights to access, correct, delete or get a copy of your personal information, and to opt out of its sale or use for targeted advertising. RVIP honors these requests from all users, whether or not a law requires it, using the process in this Section. If your state's law sets a shorter deadline or gives you more rights, we follow that law. RVIP does not sell personal information or use it for targeted advertising.

Some statutory consumer rights do not apply to employment or business-contact information or to businesses below statutory thresholds. RVIP nevertheless accepts requests and evaluates them under the laws and obligations applicable to the record. Requests ordinarily are free; any legally permitted fee or refusal for excessive requests will be explained before charging.

6 Security and incident handling

We maintain administrative, technical and physical safeguards appropriate to the information and risk, including access controls, restricted document handling and incident-response procedures. No service can guarantee absolute security. We provide legally required breach notices when the applicable conditions are met. This policy does not represent that a particular vendor eliminates all security risk.

7 Adults and communications

RVIP's service is for adults aged 18 or older and does not transport minors under its current policy. We do not knowingly establish accounts for children. Contact us if you believe a child supplied information so we can investigate and take appropriate action.

Necessary service communications are distinct from marketing. Optional marketing consent is not a condition of buying transportation, and you may use the stated unsubscribe or opt-out method. Revoking marketing consent does not stop receipts, safety notices or other necessary lawful service communications.

8 Changes and contact

We publish the effective date and provide notice of material changes as required by law. A new policy does not retroactively authorize incompatible uses requiring consent. Questions and privacy requests may be sent to info@seacoastrvip.com, identifying Privacy request in the subject. Seacoast RVIP, LLC is responsible for administering this policy. Material changes are also presented for acceptance in the apps and on the website before further booking where required.

9 Data request delivery and retention detail

The app presents Request My Data at the top of the Privacy Policy section, and the website provides the same request path where that control is available. After identity verification and preparation, the control may change to Download My Data. Availability of the download does not mean an account-deletion request has been completed.

A completed access export may be delivered as an encrypted ZIP containing a PDF guide, CSV files for readable tabular records, and JSON for structured portability where feasible. Delivery uses an authenticated link that expires. We record delivery and download events and may require renewed authentication before issuing another link.

Our internal baseline is seven years for trip, payment, refund, receipt, provider-display, and reservation-acceptance records; the relationship plus three years for driver, vehicle, and carrier qualification evidence; and permanent retention for governing records, intellectual-property assignments, adopted policy versions, and signed organizational decisions. Precise location is minimized and reviewed for deletion or de-identification when no longer needed. Legal holds, tax, insurance, safety, fraud, complaint, payment, employment, and litigation needs may require longer retention.
