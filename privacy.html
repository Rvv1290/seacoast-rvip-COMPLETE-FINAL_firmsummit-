Website Privacy Policy

seacoastrvip.com  ·  Seacoast RVIP LLC  ·  Effective October 31, 2026

This policy covers seacoastrvip.com — browsing the site, requesting a quote, booking through the web form, and the client and corporate portals.

Using the RVIP Rider or RVIP Driver app instead? Those apps collect different information, including live trip location and driver documents, and are covered by the separate App Privacy Policy.
Section 1
Information we collect

We collect the information you give us directly when you book a ride, request a quote or create a portal account:

  • Full name, telephone number and email address
  • Pickup and destination addresses, and any additional stops
  • Flight number, for airport transfers
  • Company name and billing contact, for corporate bookings
  • Payment method preference
  • Account credentials for the client and corporate portals

We also collect limited technical data — browser type, device type and pages visited — solely to keep the site working and improve it. The site does not use third-party advertising cookies or behavioural tracking.

Section 2
How we use it

We use your information only to provide and improve the service:

  • To confirm, schedule and complete your reservation
  • To send confirmations, reminders and dispatch updates
  • To process payments and issue receipts securely
  • To manage your client or corporate portal account
  • To track flight status for airport pickup accuracy
  • For accounting, invoicing and tax compliance

We do not sell, rent or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Section 3
Payment information

Payment is processed through Stripe (credit and debit card), Venmo, or cash on arrival. We do not store full card numbers or sensitive payment details on our servers — card data is tokenized and handled entirely within Stripe's PCI-compliant infrastructure. A saved payment method is kept only to bill confirmed rides to your account.

Corporate accounts billed on NET-30 terms receive consolidated statements and invoices showing fares, any card processing fee, tips and payment dates.

Section 4
Security and retention

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure or destruction. Data is stored using Firebase (Google Cloud) with access restricted to authorized personnel. No method of internet transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

We retain booking and account data for as long as necessary to provide the service and to meet legal, accounting, insurance and tax obligations. Transaction records may be retained after an account closes. You may request deletion at any time — see Delete Your Account, and the App Privacy Policy for our full retention schedule.

Section 5
Communications

We may contact you by phone, text message or email to confirm a reservation, send ride-day reminders and dispatch updates, notify you of a flight delay or schedule change, and deliver account notifications such as confirmations and invoices.

You may opt out of non-essential communications at any time by emailing info@seacoastrvip.com. Service-critical messages — ride confirmations and driver updates — cannot be opted out of while a reservation is active.

Section 6
Cookies

This site uses minimal cookies and local browser storage for functional purposes only — remembering your booking details and keeping you signed in to the portal. We do not use advertising cookies or cross-site tracking.

Section 7
Adults only

Our service is for adults aged 18 or older and we do not knowingly collect information from children or establish accounts for them. Contact us if you believe a child has provided us with personal information so we can investigate and take appropriate action.

Section 8
Your rights and changes to this policy

You may request access to, a copy of, correction of or deletion of your information by emailing info@seacoastrvip.com with "Privacy request" in the subject. Where the New Hampshire Data Privacy Act applies we respond within 45 days, with a permitted additional 45 days where reasonably necessary, and you may appeal a refusal through the same channel.

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes are posted on this page with an updated effective date, and we give notice of material changes as required by law.
