Seacoast RVIP, LLC — Terms of Service & Policies

One set for the apps and the website · Last updated: November 1, 2026

Booking here is governed by the same documents that govern the RVIP apps. In short:

  • Upfront price, no surge. Your full price is quoted before you book and includes every mandatory charge — you agree to it when you reserve. There is no surge pricing. An optional tip is up to you.
  • Your provider and vehicle, before you pay. RVIP arranges your ride and a licensed carrier — RVIP or an approved partner — provides it. The carrier of record, your driver and the vehicle are shown before payment.
  • Payment before the trip. No charge is made until a carrier accepts and you complete payment, and the trip does not begin until payment is confirmed. You receive a digital receipt.
  • Free cancellation before pickup. Cancel before pickup at no charge, with a full refund of the prepaid fare and tip. There is no cancellation or no-show fee under the current policy.
  • Adults 18+. Service is for adults; minors are not transported, even when accompanied.
